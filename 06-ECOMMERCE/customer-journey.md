# Customer journey map

Owner: Product/Ops cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL: discovery -> interest -> product understanding -> order intent -> payment/order completion ->
delivery -> wear/care -> support or repeat consideration. No conversion data is available.

| Step | Customer need | Evidence / owner to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Understand the point of view | Approved creative; Brand/Growth |
| Product | See actual fit, design and price | Verified page; Product/Ops |
| Intent | Know size, delivery and total cost | QA and service terms; Product/Ops |
| Completion | Know whether an order was accepted | Actual provider behavior; support owner |
| Delivery | Know status and how to resolve problems | Confirmed carrier process; fulfillment owner |
| Aftercare | Get useful care and exchange help | Reviewed policy and support record |

Investigate where questions recur before assuming a technical conversion issue. Separate observed events
from intent expressed in messages. Next action: walk the journey manually and log uncertainty at each transition.
