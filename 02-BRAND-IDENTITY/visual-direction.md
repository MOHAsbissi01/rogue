# Proposed visual direction

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL - NEEDS VALIDATION: dark, cinematic, urban, authentic, textural and restrained.
Use side light, visible fabric texture, real locations and controlled movement. Preserve enough exposure
to judge the actual garment. Alternate atmospheric editorial frames with neutral product evidence.

Explore original concrete, stone, metal and shadow studies; avoid copying another label's distinctive
graphics, layout or symbols. Do not assume every gothic reference is culturally interchangeable.
Keep a reference URL, creator, access date and usage classification for each inspiration item.

Three experiments: a quiet urban portrait; a precise construction-detail sequence; a small group with
individual styling. Compare interpretation and product recall, not only visual preference.
UNKNOWN: available locations, equipment, models, weather and final garment appearance.
Next action: photograph the real sample in neutral light and use those images as fidelity anchors.
