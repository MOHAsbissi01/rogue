# Typography and color exploration

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

All choices below are PROPOSALS for the visual identity. They do not define garment colors.

| Role | Proposed exploration | Review requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Near black #121212 | Preserve product detail and text contrast |
| Main text | Bone #E9E4DA | Check on dark backgrounds and small displays |
| Neutral | Ash #777777 | Do not assume it is suitable for small text |
| Limited accent | Deep red #7C2028 | Test contrast and associations; avoid reliance on color alone |

Explore a restrained gothic-influenced display face or custom lettering for short headlines,
paired with a highly readable sans-serif for body, size and checkout information.
No font is selected or licensed. Keep font names, version, source URL, permitted commercial uses,
web/print rights and license location in the rights register before installing or distributing font files.

Test 'ROGUE', numerals, TND prices, care information, French accents and any required Arabic support.
An expressive Latin face may not support Tunisian-Arabic copy; select and review an appropriate companion.
Next action: compare two typographic systems on a product page, care label and small social preview.
