# Working brand guidelines

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

Purpose: keep product, image and language consistent while the identity is developed.
FACT: the name ROGUE and gothic-inspired streetwear direction are confirmed. No logo, font, palette or
tagline is approved. All visual suggestions in this folder are PROPOSALS.

Use the approved logo directory only after recording a version-specific approval. Keep concept art editable
and separate from export files. Never reconstruct a logo from a screenshot when an authorized source exists.
Maintain readable product information even when campaign typography is expressive.

Proposed hierarchy: clear brand name; one strong visual or garment detail; concise message; specific action.
Use negative space and texture deliberately. A campaign image must not hide the fit, decoration or color a
buyer needs to assess. Actual product colors come from the verified specification, not the mood palette.

Before release verify naming, contrast, small-screen readability, garment fidelity, rights and approved copy.
UNKNOWN: final mark, minimum logo size, exclusion zone and export specifications. Set these after an actual
logo exists. Next action: review a small set of original identity concepts against the chosen positioning.
