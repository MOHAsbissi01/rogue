# Voice and tone

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL: direct, observant and confident without exaggeration. Let the detail carry the message.
FACT: founders want story and community. UNKNOWN: final messaging, language preference and approved claims.

| Situation | Proposed voice | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story | Specific image or decision grounded in truth | Invented founder hardship or grand mythology presented as history |
| Product | State measured fit, material and care once verified | Premium, perfect, indestructible or fair-price claims without evidence |
| Community | Invite participation and credit people | Pretending a movement already exists |
| Service | Clear next step, accurate timing, respectful ownership | Blaming customers or promising a result before checking |
| Launch | Exact availability and an honest action | Fake scarcity or countdowns without a real deadline |

Draft example: 'The first sample. A closer look at the details we are still refining.'
Public release requires the actual sample evidence and founder approval. Future French and Tunisian-Arabic
copy should be adapted by a fluent reviewer; record reviewer, meaning, script choice and version.
Next action: test short copy for comprehension, then collect phrases customers use with permission.
