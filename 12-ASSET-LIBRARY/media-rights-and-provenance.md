## Approval is evidence for a version and a use

Keep provenance explicit: actual product photography, retouched/composited, AI-generated, real footage, design
source or unverified. A beautiful image is not proof that it represents the garment. AI tools can change graphics,
color, proportions, fabric texture, seams or logos. Compare to accessible real references and the controlled spec.
Do not promote an image because it lives in an approved folder or was accepted by an AI provider.

## Commercial review checklist

- [ ] Exact product/sample/variant is identified; no unsupported fabric, color, fit or quality implication.
- [ ] Print artwork, placement, scale and orientation match the actual garment.
- [ ] Color is checked against physical/source references, with lighting limits noted.
- [ ] Garment proportions, construction, logo placement and anatomy are realistic and accurate.
- [ ] Creator/photographer rights and allowed editing, commercial channels, territory, duration and attribution are documented.
- [ ] Consent covers the intended model/likeness use; personal or signed documents remain in restricted storage.
- [ ] Any music, fonts, stock assets, third-party graphics and AI-tool usage rights are evidenced for this destination.
- [ ] Composite/AI provenance and any required disclosure are reviewed; an inspiration reference is not reusable artwork.
- [ ] Image/video is readable and sharp at mobile resolution; video plays fully with correct sound and captions where needed.
- [ ] Advertising suitability and truthful offer are reviewed for the actual intended platform; no live policy audit is implied.
- [ ] Reviewer records exact SHA-256, safe evidence reference, scope and review date before APPROVED.

Use [review-template.md](review-template.md) for detailed notes; summarize review_notes and approval_reference in
the manifest. A rights check alone does not establish product accuracy. Publishing still requires explicit founder
authorization, and actual publication evidence is separate. Changed source bytes reset approval to REVIEW on scan.
No automated check can certify legal permissions or physical fidelity.

## Privacy and backup

Keep customer identifiers, signed agreements, identity files, confidential supplier material and secrets outside
the library and Git. The server is local/read-only and filters known sensitive names, but cannot recognize all
private content inside an innocently named photo. Review every import. Private GitHub and OneDrive are not secure
access management or independent media backups. Record restoration evidence before asserting a backup exists.
