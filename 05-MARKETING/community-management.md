# Community management

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL process: listen, clarify, respond accurately and protect participants. Assign a moderator and a
reviewed response window before making a public service promise. No current response-time claim is established.

For product questions use the approved specification; for missing details say they are being confirmed.
For order problems move identifying information to an approved private support route, never a Git file or
public comment. Summarize themes anonymously. Remove personal data from public-facing records.

Credit contributors only with their permission. Moderate harassment consistently, document serious incidents
privately and avoid arguments designed to increase engagement. Do not repost a private message as social proof
without specific consent. Gifted/paid collaborations need disclosure review.
Next action: choose the support owner, escalation process and a few truthful response drafts before launch.
