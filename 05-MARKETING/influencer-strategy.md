# Creator and influencer strategy

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL: choose collaborators for audience relevance, style fit, communication and rights clarity before
follower count. No creator has been selected, contacted or promised compensation.

Review recent public work, actual audience context if willingly supplied, comment relevance and garment
presentation. Request clear deliverables, revision scope, publication dates, payment/gift terms and separate
organic/paid usage rights. Do not assume a creator's music license permits brand advertising.

Pilot a small collaboration only after product readiness and a concrete founder-approved budget. Capture
creator consent, disclosure plan, attribution method and allowed reuse period. Store agreements and identity
documents privately; keep only anonymized codes and safe terms in the workspace.
Success depends on the objective: product questions, relevant visits, qualified interest or contribution from
actual orders. Next action: define a selection rubric and brief before any outreach or spending.
