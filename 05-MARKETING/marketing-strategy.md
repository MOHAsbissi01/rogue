# Proposed marketing strategy

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Purpose and known limits

Help people understand the brand and garment, then learn whether that understanding leads to meaningful
interest and viable orders. FACT: an Instagram URL and store URL were supplied. No channel audit, metrics,
customer validation or approved budget exists in this record.

## Proposed sequence

Begin with organic creative validation: truthful sample/process stories, visible product details and a
small number of distinct concepts. Ask practical questions about interpretation, fit and objections.
Once product readiness, store QA and economics are documented, prepare a product reveal and conversion offer.
Only then consider a bounded paid experiment with explicit founder authorization.

Use the five proposed content pillars as a testable editorial system, not a quota. Test ages 17-28 as an
audience hypothesis and 29-35 secondarily; actual advertising eligibility and targeting require review at setup.
Avoid turning the broad 14-35 intent into automatic paid targeting of minors.

## Funnel and decisions

Awareness asks whether the idea is noticed and understood. Engagement asks whether people save, share or
respond with useful questions. Reveal asks whether viewers understand the actual hoodie. Conversion asks
whether an accurate offer and working journey result in completed orders at sustainable contribution.
These are different questions; do not select a sales creative solely on reach.

UNKNOWN: resource capacity, baseline performance, channel preference and allowable acquisition cost.
Next action: approve two concept pilots and a comparable observation window. Predeclare the learning question,
record actual results and objections, and revise before committing substantial advertising spend.
