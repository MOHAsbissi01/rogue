# C06 - The hoodie, clearly

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

**PROPOSAL - not approved or produced.** Stage: Conversion - gated. Product reference: RG-H001 only after its visual/spec reference is verified.

## Hook and narrative

Hook: Here is what you need to decide.

15-30-second narrative: 0-3s: an unobscured real garment view. 3-10s: front/back and verified detail. 10-18s: accurate size-guide and delivery information. 18-24s: approved price/availability and a real store CTA, only after launch authorization.

## Visual and production brief

Visuals: Clean product evidence with restrained dark identity framing. Screen inserts use actual approved storefront captures after manual QA.

Shot list: Front; back; decoration; fit; real size guide; approved service card; verified offer and CTA.

Product appearance requirements: Use approved production-representative footage. No false discount, stock quantity, countdown or free-shipping claim. Block production of final offer cards until inputs exist.

Audio direction: Clear licensed low-distraction bed, optional approved factual voiceover. Check license for paid use separately.

CTA: View sizes and order only when the offer and launch are approved.

Platform: Instagram organic first; optional Meta paid test after authorization.

Deliverables: One 24-second vertical edit, two hook variants, a still offer card and caption variants with the same truthful offer.

## Validation and release

Success measures: Use measurable product visits, add-to-cart/order intent and completed orders. Judge contribution after acquisition and matured returns; ROAS alone cannot establish viability.

Execution options: real-world capture is the fidelity source; an editor may assemble and animate cleared assets.
AI/Higgsfield may be considered for non-product atmosphere with permission-cleared references and authorized spend.
Never assume a generated garment is accurate. Require frame-level design review or replace it with real footage.

Before production assign an owner, actual duration, reference version, rights/consent codes, budget and reviewer.
Before publication clear music for the actual channel, participant use, branded-content disclosure where relevant,
all claims and explicit release authorization. No results or benchmark thresholds are prefilled.
Next action: decide whether this concept answers a current learning question and can be produced truthfully.
