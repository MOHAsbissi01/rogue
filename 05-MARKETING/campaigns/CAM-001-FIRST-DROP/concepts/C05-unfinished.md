## Hook and narrative

Hook: This is the first sample. Here is what we still need to learn.

15-30-second narrative: 0-4s: show the real sample and a truthful development caption. 4-12s: a founder describes one documented open question. 12-22s: show the intended inspection or measurement without claiming a result. 22-30s: invite one practical question.

## Visual and production brief

Visuals: Honest desk or fitting space, original notes and readable details. A founder can remain off camera if preferred.

Shot list: Sample on table; original note; measured point; detail under review; founder hands or consented portrait; closing question.

Product appearance requirements: State prototype status clearly. Only show actual observations; do not present planned tests as performed or hide a relevant revision.

Audio direction: Natural voice with consent and minimal original room tone. Subtitle factual speech accurately.

CTA: What would you want to know before choosing a hoodie?

Platform: Instagram Reel.

Deliverables: One 30-second voiced or caption-led edit, a 15-second excerpt and three stills.
