# C04 - One piece, different presence

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

**PROPOSAL - not approved or produced.** Stage: Lifestyle / engagement. Product reference: RG-H001 only after its visual/spec reference is verified.

## Hook and narrative

Hook: A shared piece can carry different meanings.

15-30-second narrative: 0-3s: two consenting adult participants enter separate frames. 3-11s: each wears the actual sample in their own styling turn. 11-21s: split sequence shows different movement and expression. 21-28s: ask how the viewer would style it.

## Visual and production brief

Visuals: Simple matching locations and framing with individuality in movement. Shoot sequentially if only one sample exists.

Shot list: Portrait A; portrait B; front turn each; back view each; true fit detail; two closing frames.

Product appearance requirements: One sample does not prove a size range. Do not stretch or reshape the hoodie in editing. State only verified sample/fit context.

Audio direction: Licensed rhythmic instrumental with editable quiet sections. Obtain separate usage consent for each participant.

CTA: How would you make it your own?

Platform: Instagram Reel and Stories response prompt.

Deliverables: One 28-second edit, two short portrait crops, three story frames and a cover still.

## Validation and release

Success measures: Assess styling responses, relevant shares and actual participant/viewer interpretation. Do not claim inclusive fit from two people.

Execution options: real-world capture is the fidelity source; an editor may assemble and animate cleared assets.
AI/Higgsfield may be considered for non-product atmosphere with permission-cleared references and authorized spend.
Never assume a generated garment is accurate. Require frame-level design review or replace it with real footage.

Before production assign an owner, actual duration, reference version, rights/consent codes, budget and reviewer.
Before publication clear music for the actual channel, participant use, branded-content disclosure where relevant,
all claims and explicit release authorization. No results or benchmark thresholds are prefilled.
Next action: decide whether this concept answers a current learning question and can be produced truthfully.
