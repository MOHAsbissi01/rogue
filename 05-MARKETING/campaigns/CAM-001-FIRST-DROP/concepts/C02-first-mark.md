**PROPOSAL - not approved or produced.** Stage: Engagement. Product reference: RG-H001 only after its visual/spec reference is verified.

## Hook and narrative

Hook: Which detail would you notice first?

15-30-second narrative: 0-3s: close view of an actual original sketch detail. 3-8s: match cut to the same feature on the real sample. 8-18s: hands point to two genuine choices still under review. 18-25s: ask one specific preference question.

## Visual and production brief

Visuals: Tabletop paper and real fabric, directional light and honest working marks. Only show process files that actually exist.

Shot list: Original source page; matching garment detail; neutral front; hands tracing placement; two factual option cards; closing question.

Product appearance requirements: Never invent embroidery/print production footage or claim an unperformed technique. Keep the sample art legible and unchanged.

Audio direction: Paper/fabric handling recorded on set; optional licensed minimal rhythm under clear captions.

CTA: Tell us which detail catches your eye, and why.

Platform: Instagram Reel and supporting carousel.

Deliverables: One 25-second edit, a 5-frame process carousel, two alternative question captions.

## Validation and release

Success measures: Evaluate relevant replies, saves per reach and specificity of feedback. Separate curiosity from purchase intent; no prefilled threshold.

Execution options: real-world capture is the fidelity source; an editor may assemble and animate cleared assets.
AI/Higgsfield may be considered for non-product atmosphere with permission-cleared references and authorized spend.
Never assume a generated garment is accurate. Require frame-level design review or replace it with real footage.

Before production assign an owner, actual duration, reference version, rights/consent codes, budget and reviewer.
Before publication clear music for the actual channel, participant use, branded-content disclosure where relevant,
all claims and explicit release authorization. No results or benchmark thresholds are prefilled.
Next action: decide whether this concept answers a current learning question and can be produced truthfully.
