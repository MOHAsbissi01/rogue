# C01 - Afterhours signal

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

**PROPOSAL - not approved or produced.** Stage: Awareness. Product reference: RG-H001 only after its visual/spec reference is verified.

## Hook and narrative

Hook: A small movement emerges from a still city frame.

15-30-second narrative: 0-3s: a motionless textured wall and a step into frame. 3-9s: a slow walk reveals a real sleeve detail. 9-18s: front/back movement in a readable pool of light. 18-24s: the wearer pauses and the brand invitation appears.

## Visual and production brief

Visuals: Restrained urban framing, deep shadows with readable cloth, one real location. No invented crowd or dangerous staging.

Shot list: Wide establishing frame; shoe/step crop; sleeve detail; medium front turn; full back; still final portrait.

Product appearance requirements: Show the actual front and back and preserve graphic placement. The darkness must not conceal fit or alter perceived garment color.

Audio direction: Original ambient city sound and a licensed sparse pulse. No copyrighted track assumed cleared.

CTA: Follow the development of ROGUE.

Platform: Instagram Reel; optional separately reviewed TikTok edit.

Deliverables: One 24-second vertical edit, two 3-second hook variants, one cover still, one silent captioned review.

## Validation and release

Success measures: Compare engaged-view definition/retention where available, shares per reach and actual brand/product recall comments. Record platform limits.

Execution options: real-world capture is the fidelity source; an editor may assemble and animate cleared assets.
AI/Higgsfield may be considered for non-product atmosphere with permission-cleared references and authorized spend.
Never assume a generated garment is accurate. Require frame-level design review or replace it with real footage.

Before production assign an owner, actual duration, reference version, rights/consent codes, budget and reviewer.
Before publication clear music for the actual channel, participant use, branded-content disclosure where relevant,
all claims and explicit release authorization. No results or benchmark thresholds are prefilled.
Next action: decide whether this concept answers a current learning question and can be produced truthfully.
