# C03 - A closer look

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

**PROPOSAL - not approved or produced.** Stage: Product reveal. Product reference: RG-H001 only after its visual/spec reference is verified.

## Hook and narrative

Hook: Before the story, look at the garment.

15-30-second narrative: 0-3s: neutral macro of real texture. 3-9s: full front and back. 9-18s: three actual construction/design details, each labeled descriptively. 18-27s: natural movement and invitation to inspect verified details.

## Visual and production brief

Visuals: Controlled honest light, slow camera and neutral background. Editorial restraint with enough exposure for inspection.

Shot list: Fabric macro; full front; full back; seam detail; real decoration; fit movement; product-detail CTA.

Product appearance requirements: Use only real footage for factual garment views. Do not imply softness, GSM or durability from appearance. Captions must match verified spec.

Audio direction: Recorded cloth movement and a cleared subtle bed; no dramatic sound implying a test result.

CTA: See the verified product details when the page is ready.

Platform: Instagram Reel; product-page video after approval.

Deliverables: One 27-second edit, a clean product-view cut, three detail stills and a caption file for editor use.

## Validation and release

Success measures: Track saves, specific product questions and product-page visits when measurable. Compare whether questions become more informed, not just fewer.

Execution options: real-world capture is the fidelity source; an editor may assemble and animate cleared assets.
AI/Higgsfield may be considered for non-product atmosphere with permission-cleared references and authorized spend.
Never assume a generated garment is accurate. Require frame-level design review or replace it with real footage.

Before production assign an owner, actual duration, reference version, rights/consent codes, budget and reviewer.
Before publication clear music for the actual channel, participant use, branded-content disclosure where relevant,
all claims and explicit release authorization. No results or benchmark thresholds are prefilled.
Next action: decide whether this concept answers a current learning question and can be produced truthfully.
