# Product development workflow

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL stages: brief -> specification -> sample -> measured fit and quality review -> revision ->
cost/price review -> production authorization -> incoming inspection -> launch approval -> customer learning.

At each stage name the owner, exact sample/art version, open questions, evidence and reviewer. A sample can
return to revision without losing its earlier record. Physical production requires both founders' explicit
quantity and spend approval; a script only creates records.

Before ordering, compare an approved physical sample with the controlled specification. Confirm supplier
terms, rights, quality tolerances, delivery expectations and cash timing in writing. At receipt separate
saleable, reserved and quarantined units, then reconcile counts before the store shows availability.

FACT: only the first physical prototype's existence is confirmed. No downstream stage is complete.
Next action: identify its physical revision and gather enough information to begin a meaningful review.
