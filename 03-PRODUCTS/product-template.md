# Product onboarding checklist

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

Use the onboarding script to create the folder and catalog row. This checklist explains what to complete.

1. Identity: organizational ID, collection, base/variant SKU, commercial name and accountable owner.
2. Story: intended audience need, design explanation, references and rights classification.
3. Specification: fabric, composition, GSM evidence, fit, measurements, tolerances, color and size variants.
4. Supply: supplier code, source of quote, validity, sample versions and confirmed production terms.
5. Validation: actual quality tests, fit observations, revisions and exact approval references.
6. Economics: landed unit cost, retail proposal, contribution and margin under complete sourced assumptions.
7. Operations: counted saleable stock, packaging, care, fulfillment and launch status.
8. Assets: source art, front/back/details/fit, raw video, selected and approved derivatives; backup and rights evidence.

UNKNOWN is the correct initial value for unavailable facts. Use unlimited dated albums and sample revisions.
Do not copy a previous product's specifications or approval into a new one. Next action: complete RG-H001
before using its record as a source of production information.
