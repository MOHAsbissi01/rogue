# Sizing and fit method

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

No size range or measurements are approved. Establish a repeatable measurement method before publishing
a chart. Use centimeters, flat measurements, explicit landmarks and garment condition (before/after wash).

Agree chest width, length, shoulder, sleeve, hem, cuff and hood points as applicable. Take repeat readings
and record who measured. Distinguish body measurements from garment measurements. Fit descriptions should
explain intended ease and wearing behavior only after observing real samples.

PROPOSAL: conduct consented fit sessions across the intended range of needs, use anonymous participant codes,
and note movement, layering and comfort. Avoid implying a limited sample guarantees fit for everyone.
Keep personal identity records outside Git. Approve grading and tolerances with the supplier before ordering.
Next action: complete the current sample's sizing sheet and photograph the measurement landmarks.
