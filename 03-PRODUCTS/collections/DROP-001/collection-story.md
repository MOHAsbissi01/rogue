# DROP-001 story development

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

No public collection story is approved. FACT: a first physical prototype exists. Its creation history has
not been supplied. PROPOSAL: tell the real sequence from idea to sample, showing decisions and open questions.

Draft structure: a founder's truthful motivation -> one original design choice -> the physical sample ->
what is being tested next -> an invitation to follow progress. Fill each beat with actual evidence.
Do not invent a cultural movement, production milestone or personal struggle to make the story dramatic.

Next action: interview both founders privately, compare recollections with original files and write a short
approved account. A campaign can use atmosphere, but factual captions must stay grounded in the record.
