# Manufacturing quality checklist

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

Status: proposed process; no inspection or test has been performed for this setup.

- [ ] Controlled specification and approved physical sample match.
- [ ] Fabric and decoration evidence, color references, measurements and tolerances are agreed.
- [ ] Incoming inspection sample/coverage plan is approved for the actual order size and risks.
- [ ] Construction, fit, measurements, decoration, labels and packaging are checked with recorded results.
- [ ] Agreed wash/wear checks have documented conditions, observations and comparison photos.
- [ ] Defect severity, quarantine, rework/reject process and supplier remedy are agreed in writing.
- [ ] Counts reconcile: received, accepted, reserved, quarantined and rejected.
- [ ] Actual reviewer signs the batch release; unresolved material issues stop release.

Do not invent inspection thresholds or claim certified testing. Agree methods and acceptance criteria before
testing. Next action: review the product-specific QC sheet and determine which checks need outside expertise.
