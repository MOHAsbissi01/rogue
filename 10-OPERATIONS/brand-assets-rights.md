# Brand assets and rights

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

No license or rights clearance is established by this workspace. Original ROGUE work, third-party references,
commissioned work, music, fonts, photos and AI references all need traceable permitted-use records.

For each asset record creator/owner (safe role or code), source URL, original path/version, rights basis,
organic/paid/product use, territories, duration, edits, attribution, consent code, expiry and evidence location.
Store private agreements, identity files and signatures outside Git. A private link/reference code can identify
restricted evidence without copying its contents into the repository.

Do not treat public references as reusable campaign art. Do not assume commissioning work transfers every right,
or that a platform music library permits every commercial use. Verify current terms when an actual use is planned.
For creators review gifted/paid branded-content disclosure and likeness use, including any synthetic processing.
Next action: inventory actual assets and rights gaps before approving design or publication.
