Use this checklist to obtain a comparable written response for one exact sample/specification and quantity.

## Requested quotation details

Record answer, included/excluded status, amount/basis in TND, source date, validity and unresolved question for each line.
Blank answers remain UNKNOWN. A zero or 'included' needs written confirmation.

- [ ] Exact hoodie/blank garment cost: supplied blank versus cut-and-sew; labor, fabric and trims included; saleable-unit basis.
- [ ] Graphics/art preparation: artwork conversion, digitization, screens/setup and repeat-order fees, separately from production.
- [ ] Printing: process, placements, dimensions, colors, per-unit cost and setup cost for each proposed design.
- [ ] Embroidery: placements, dimensions/stitch basis, digitization/setup and per-unit cost; identify whether combined with printing.
- [ ] Fabric specification: composition, knit/construction, GSM, finish, material source and supporting evidence; substitutions approval.
- [ ] Size variations: available sizes, measurement/grade chart, tolerances and any larger-size surcharges or per-size constraints.
- [ ] Colors/design variations: available materials, color consistency, mixed designs/sizes in one order and impact on price and timing.
- [ ] Quantity policy: confirm no minimum quantity in writing for the quoted process; distinguish total-order, per-design and per-size limits.
- [ ] Discounts: exact quantity breaks, conditions, validity and whether sample/decoration charges are excluded; do not assume eligibility.
- [ ] Sample fees: first sample, revisions, replacement samples, refund/credit conditions and whether samples match production materials.
- [ ] Packaging and labels: individual packaging, care/brand labels, label setup and cartons; itemize supplied-by-brand components.
- [ ] Delivery/transport: origin/destination basis, inbound freight, handling, collection option, timing and who bears loss/damage costs.
- [ ] Tax treatment: whether each amount includes tax, applicable tax basis and required invoice details; obtain local advice where needed.
- [ ] Lead times: sample and production durations, start trigger (deposit/art/spec approval), material availability and revision delays.
- [ ] Payment and quote validity: deposit/balance schedule, currency, expiry, cancellation/change terms and price-change conditions.
- [ ] Quality and remedies: approved sample/spec reference, inspection access, defects/rework/replacement procedure and acceptance process.
- [ ] Rights: permission to manufacture supplied art, confidentiality and restrictions on reuse; store signed documents privately.

## Decision gate

Reconcile each line with the landed-cost formula: production + decoration + packaging + inbound freight + allocated
production overhead. Resolve bundled inclusions before adding costs. Keep sample/setup charges on a documented
fixed or allocated basis, never both. Compare designs on equivalent tax, size and quantity assumptions.
Both founders must record a specific spending approval before any sample or batch purchase; a completed checklist
alone is not approval. Keep supplier-confidential originals, contacts and payment details outside Git.
