# Legal and compliance research checklist

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

This is an unverified research checklist, not legal advice or a statement of current Tunisian law.
No registration, filing, tax position or compliance status is asserted.

Obtain qualified local review for business/entity setup, tax/invoicing, consumer information and returns,
textile/care/origin labeling, ecommerce/privacy, handling of minors, marketing disclosures, intellectual property,
creator/model agreements, supplier terms and import/export issues if applicable. Do not invent required rates,
deadlines, registration classes or policy periods.

For each topic record applicability, official evidence source and date, adviser/reviewer, action, owner,
deadline if verified and actual completion evidence. Keep confidential correspondence and identifying filings
outside Git. Next action: define the intended operating model and arrange a current-source professional review
before publishing policies or entering commitments.
