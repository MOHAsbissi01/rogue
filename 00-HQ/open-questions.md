# Founder decision questions

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

Answer one cluster per weekly meeting. Record the choice, rejected options, evidence, owner and revisit date.
All answers below remain UNKNOWN.

| Topic | Decision question | Evidence needed | Gate affected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Who is the first buyer and in which wearing situation? | Interviews and observed buying history | Positioning |
| Meaning | Which aspect of gothic style belongs to ROGUE, and which feels borrowed? | Founder intent, customer interpretation | Identity |
| Offer | What would make a buyer choose this hoodie over an existing option? | Comparable garments and specific buyer objections | USP |
| Product | What fit, fabric and decoration can the actual sample prove? | Measurements, material records, test notes | Product |
| Price | Which price covers variable costs and feels justified to the chosen buyer? | Dated quotes, contribution model, price interviews | Commercial |
| Supply | What minimum order, lead time and defect remedy are confirmed in writing? | Supplier evidence kept privately | Production |
| Cash | What cash can founders risk, and what reserve must remain? | Private cash position and founder decision | Spending |
| Community | What recurring activity would people join without a discount? | Small consented pilot, repeat participation | Community |
| Store | Who owns checkout, delivery, returns and escalation? | Manual QA and verified provider terms | Launch |
| Rights | Who owns artwork and where are use permissions stored? | Rights register, private agreements | Creative |
| Roles | Who executes and who reviews each decision? | Availability and explicit acceptance | Operations |
| Success | What result would cause us to revise or stop? | A predeclared test objective and actual data | Learning |

Recommended first action: select at most three questions for the first meeting and assign evidence
collection before making irreversible commitments. An unanswered question is a tracked blocker, not a failure.
