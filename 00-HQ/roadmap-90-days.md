# 90-day roadmap

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL: 12 working weeks followed by six days of buffer/review. Day 1 is the founders' approved start
date (UNKNOWN), not an automatic commitment from the document date. Weeks may extend when evidence is missing.

| Week | Focus and concrete output | Proposed owner | Exit gate before next commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Inventory existing research; measure/photo sample; assign roles | Both | Sources located, unknowns logged, responsibilities accepted |
| 2 | Interview likely buyers; begin 5-10 competitor evidence rows | Brand/Growth | Evidence covers motives, objections and alternatives, not only friends' praise |
| 3 | Compare positioning options; draft identity and story | Both | Written choice of hypothesis to test, with limits and revisit date |
| 4 | Build and review brand kit; check artwork/font rights | Brand/Growth | Legibility, ownership and use rules approved before external creative use |
| 5 | Fit, wash and decoration tests; sample revision decision | Product/Ops | Test methods, results and unresolved faults documented; no untested quality claim |
| 6 | Quotes, landed cost, order contribution and cash scenarios | Both | Positive contribution under approved assumptions plus cash reserve; price not inferred |
| 7 | Mobile storefront QA and provider/fulfillment confirmation | Product/Ops | Critical checkout, size, shipping and policy issues closed before traffic spend |
| 8 | Approved production plan or revised sample; inspect first output | Product/Ops | Quantity/cost/rights/spec approved and cash gate met before supplier payment |
| 9 | Organic story and craft concepts; collect feedback | Brand/Growth | Fidelity and rights cleared; compare actual responses in like observation windows |
| 10 | Launch rehearsal, actual stock reconciliation and support readiness | Both | Saleable stock or accurate lead-time terms, fulfillment and launch approvals recorded |
| 11 | Launch if ready; optional small controlled advertising test | Both | Explicit spend ceiling, economics, consent/rights and measurable events verified |
| 12 | Reconcile orders, costs, returns and creative lessons | Both | Evidence-based continue/revise/stop decision; no scaling from vanity metrics alone |

Days 85-90: close reporting gaps, allow delayed delivery/return outcomes to mature, review risks and
approve the next cycle. Do not compress sample or fulfillment checks to meet a calendar date.

## Spending gates

G1 Research: agree purpose, owner and maximum affordable spend before any paid research or tools.
G2 Sample: approve specification and revision scope before sample expenditure.
G3 Production: verify signed-off sample, quotes, rights, demand evidence, unit economics, quantity and cash reserve.
G4 Promotion: clear critical store issues, approve assets, establish reliable measurement and an explicit loss ceiling.
G5 Scale: reconcile contribution after acquisition and matured returns; expand only within a fresh approved budget.
None of these gates is complete or authorized by this draft. A founder decision must name the amount, scope and expiry.
