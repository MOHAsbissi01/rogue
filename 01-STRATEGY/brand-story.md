# Three alternative brand-story directions

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

FACT: ROGUE is Tunisian, has two cofounders and an existing first hoodie prototype. Their personal origin
story and reasons for choosing the name are UNKNOWN. Never invent childhood memories, struggles or milestones.

## Direction 1: after the city quiets - PROPOSAL

Theme: the moment everyday noise falls away and personal expression becomes clearer.
Narrative: ordinary movement through an urban setting, a pause, then a deliberate choice of how to show up.
Product role: the actual hoodie belongs in the routine, not as a magical transformation.
Proof needed: real founder perspective, original location footage and an approved garment.
Question: does this reflect the founders' experience or only an attractive visual style?

## Direction 2: the first mark - PROPOSAL

Theme: an idea taking physical shape through decisions and revisions.
Narrative: sketches and material investigation lead to the existing sample, with unresolved choices shown honestly.
Product role: reveal how specific design decisions change appearance and wear.
Proof needed: actual dated working files and real sample notes; do not stage invented craftsmanship history.
Question: what process evidence can be shown without disclosing confidential supplier information?

## Direction 3: a circle with room - PROPOSAL

Theme: a shared taste leaves space for different personalities.
Narrative: consenting participants interpret a visual prompt in their own way, then connect through one common piece.
Product role: common thread, with truthful fit on each person; avoid claiming a broad community already exists.
Proof needed: participant consent, original contributions and a real activity people chose to join.
Question: what can people do together before buying anything?

Recommended action: collect a recorded or written founder account privately, extract verifiable passages,
and choose a direction whose evidence already exists. Treat all public copy as draft until approved.
