# Community strategy and identity alternatives

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Purpose

Build participation people value beyond transactions. FACT: community is an aspiration; no active membership
or engagement numbers were supplied. Names below are alternatives, not approved identities or cleared trademarks.

| Proposed identity | Shared idea | First activity to test | Risk to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ROGUE Afterhours | Personal expression in quieter city moments | Consented original night-texture photo prompt | Excluding people who do not go out at night |
| ROGUE Atelier | Making and refining creative work | Sketch/detail discussion using original work | Implying manufacturing expertise not yet established |
| ROGUE Circle | Belonging while keeping individual style | Small moderated styling/story exchange | Generic name with little reason to return |

## Operating proposal

Pilot one repeatable activity through the confirmed Instagram channel only after founder authorization to
publish. Ask permission before reposting, credit creators, document use scope, and avoid storing participant
identities in Git. Set moderation standards against harassment and personal data exposure.

## Learn before expanding

Track actual voluntary participation, repeat participation and qualitative feedback in comparable periods.
Likes do not establish community health. Ask whether members would repeat the activity without a giveaway.
UNKNOWN: moderation capacity, preferred language, activity interest and acceptable frequency.
Next action: compare the three identities in interviews, then choose a small pilot with an accepted moderator.
Do not open additional communities until responsibility and recurring activity are credible.
