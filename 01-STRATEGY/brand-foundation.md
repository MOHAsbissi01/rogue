# Proposed brand foundation

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Purpose and starting point

Build a coherent reason for ROGUE to exist that the hoodie, stories and customer experience can support.
FACT: the founders want community, a recognizable mindset, storytelling and good quality at a fair price.
UNKNOWN: which people will value this combination enough to buy and return.

## Foundation to test - PROPOSAL

- Human tension: wanting a visible personal identity while still finding people who understand it.
- Intended audience: test 17-28 first and 29-35 second; this is not demographic validation.
- Brand role: provide wearable self-expression and a shared space for people drawn to darker street culture.
- Product promise to earn: a hoodie whose fit, construction and design justify its eventual price.
- Emotional territory: quiet defiance, belonging by choice, creative independence.
- Character: deliberate, grounded and expressive. Avoid theatrical intimidation or claims of superiority.

## Proof required before claims

Link quality language to named tests and the actual production garment. Link price language to comparable
offers and complete economics. Link community language to repeat participation and member consent.
Founders' aspirations alone cannot substantiate those claims.

## Boundaries and decisions

Do not borrow religious symbols or subculture imagery merely as decoration without understanding their
meaning and audience interpretation. Do not make alienation a condition for belonging or use shame to sell.
Choose one positioning alternative after interviews, and ask whether the actual prototype expresses it.
Next action: each founder independently writes why the first hoodie should exist, then compare answers.
