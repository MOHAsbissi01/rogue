# Tunisia market-entry working plan

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Scope and evidence boundary

FACT: ROGUE is based in Tunisia and has user-supplied Instagram and Converty shop URLs.
No Tunisian market size, competitor price, purchasing behavior, delivery capability or legal requirement
has been researched for this workspace. All commercial operating assumptions remain NEEDS VALIDATION.

## Proposed entry sequence

Start with one clearly explained hoodie offer and a manageable fulfillment scope. Choose geographic coverage
only after comparing actual delivery quotes, service terms and support capacity. Do not assume all regions
can be served at one cost or that a particular payment method is available.

Investigate payment options, rejected/refused deliveries, exchange logistics, language preference and trust
signals through provider evidence and buyer interviews. Document costs and delivery timing as observed inputs.
Use TND for domestic planning. Verify taxes and business requirements with a qualified local adviser before trading.

## Evidence questions

- Which alternatives do buyers actually compare, at what recorded prices and dates?
- Which garment details and service assurances make the offer understandable?
- What fulfillment areas and payment methods can providers support in writing?
- Which English, French or Tunisian-Arabic copy best explains the offer without distorting the brand?
- Can the founders support returns, delivery problems and questions with current capacity?

Recommended action: build 5-10 competitor records, interview potential buyers, obtain provider terms,
and complete store QA before public launch promises. Do not treat social audience size as market demand.
