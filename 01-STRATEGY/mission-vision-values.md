# Mission, vision and values

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Proposed mission

Create gothic-influenced streetwear that helps people express themselves, with thoughtful construction,
honest information and stories they can participate in. This is a direction to approve, not a current guarantee.

## Proposed vision

Grow ROGUE into a recognizable Tunisian creative community whose clothing and conduct share the same point
of view. No scale, geography expansion or timeline has been approved.

## Values as observable behavior - PROPOSAL

| Value | What it means in practice | Evidence to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-expression | Offer creative space without telling people who they must be | Customer language and diverse use cases |
| Craft and honesty | Show actual materials, fit and limitations | Specs, test notes and accurate product pages |
| Fair exchange | Explain the offer and treat complaints consistently | Economics review and anonymized resolution notes |
| Belonging | Ask for permission, listen and credit contributors | Consent status and recurring participation |
| Independence | Develop original work and acknowledge references | Source and rights register |

FACT: these values respond to the stated aspirations. UNKNOWN: founders' priorities when values conflict.
Resolve a real tradeoff at the meeting: if a decoration detail raises cost, do we simplify, reprice or postpone?
Approve a short mission only after each founder can explain what it changes in product and service decisions.
