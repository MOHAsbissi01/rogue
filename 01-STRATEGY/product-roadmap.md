# Product roadmap

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Known starting point

FACT: the launch category is hoodies and a first physical prototype exists. RG-H001 is the internal record
for it; DROP-001 is an organizational collection. Final commercial names, colorways, sizing and quantities are UNKNOWN.

## Proposed sequence

1. Document RG-H001: link original art, measured specification, actual sample version and intended use.
2. Validate fit, fabric, construction and decoration through a defined protocol; resolve failures or revise.
3. Compare production feasibility and cost before selecting variants. Do not add variants solely for visual variety.
4. Approve a production sample, commercial offer and fulfillment process before ordering inventory.
5. Use actual customer feedback and contribution to decide whether to repeat, refine or expand.

Future products remain an idea backlog, not a commitment. Evaluate any idea against audience need,
brand relevance, shared production capability, sample risk, cash requirement and operational complexity.
Do not populate fictitious future SKUs as if they are available stock.

## Decision questions

What must the first hoodie teach us? Which defect would stop production? How many variants can the team
inspect and fulfill accurately? What evidence would justify a second product? Next action: complete the current
specification and sample review before expanding the range.
