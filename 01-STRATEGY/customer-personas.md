# Audience hypotheses, not validated personas

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

FACT: the broad stated audience is 14-35. PROPOSAL: prioritize testing 17-28, with 29-35 as secondary.
These are research recruitment hypotheses. Do not invent occupations, incomes, cities or buying frequency.

| Hypothesis | Possible job the hoodie does | Questions to test | What would challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: expressive dresser, 17-28 | Makes a personal style legible in daily wear | Where would you wear this? What feels excessive? | Preference only for shoot imagery, not the garment |
| B: creative participant, 17-28 | Connects clothing with music/art and people | Which activities do you already join? Why return? | Interest depends entirely on discounts |
| C: deliberate buyer, 29-35 | Adds a distinctive piece without sacrificing usability | Which fit, care and construction details matter? | Product style or service fails practical needs |

UNKNOWN for every hypothesis: location, spending power, purchase authority, fit needs, sizes, preferred language
and channels. Age alone does not identify a niche. Recruit across different prior buying experiences.

Use adult participants for the initial operational pilot as a proposed scope choice. The broad audience includes
minors; any later participation involving them needs a reviewed consent and privacy process. This document does
not establish legal requirements or authorize targeted advertising to minors.

After interviews, replace assumptions with anonymized patterns, exceptions and sample limitations. Never treat
a handful of interviews as statistically representative demand. Next action: choose recruitment criteria based
on clothing behavior and style interest, then ask about recent purchases rather than imagined loyalty.
