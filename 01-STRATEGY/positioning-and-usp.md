# Positioning alternatives and USP development

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

All three statements below are **PROPOSAL - NEEDS VALIDATION**. None is an approved USP or proven claim.

## Alternative A: everyday dark expression

For people in Tunisia drawn to darker visual culture, ROGUE aims to offer gothic-influenced streetwear
that makes self-expression part of everyday dressing, supported by thoughtful fit and construction.
Test: does the garment feel wearable beyond a shoot, and is the design distinctive without a large logo?
Risk: too broad to explain why a buyer should choose it.

## Alternative B: a story you can wear

For people who connect clothing with identity, ROGUE proposes story-led hoodie drops that invite them
into an evolving creative world, with each design connected to an intentional narrative.
Test: can people recall the story and connect it to the real design after seeing the product?
Risk: a strong film could conceal an ordinary product; validate product preference separately.

## Alternative C: belonging on your own terms

For people seeking a creative community around darker street style, ROGUE proposes clothing and shared
rituals that support individual expression and participation without requiring conformity.
Test: would someone join an activity without a purchase incentive, and return voluntarily?
Risk: calling customers a community before mutual participation exists.

## Build a defensible USP

Compare target need, competitor alternatives, specific benefit, reason to believe and evidence gaps.
A formal USP requires a difference the actual garment or experience can demonstrate. 'Quality at a fair
price' remains a goal and cannot serve as proof by itself. UNKNOWN: price, manufacturing capability and buyer need.
Next action: test these statements without brand artwork, then test the prototype separately to expose mismatch.
Choose an option only after founders review both preference and objections; record rejection reasons too.
