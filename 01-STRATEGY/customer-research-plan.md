# Customer research plan

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Decision to support

Understand who values the prototype, what prevents purchase and which offer deserves further testing.
FACT: no customer validation has been supplied. PROPOSAL: begin with 8-12 voluntary adult interviews,
including people outside the founders' close circle; this is a practical qualitative sample, not a market estimate.

## Method

1. Agree recruitment criteria, voluntary consent, note retention and anonymized IDs before contacting anyone.
2. Ask about a recent hoodie purchase: alternatives, actual spend if willingly shared, fit and disappointment.
3. Show the real prototype with neutral lighting before presenting polished campaign imagery.
4. Ask for first interpretation, intended wearing context and what would stop purchase.
5. Show the three positioning statements in varied order. Ask for recall and reasons, not only preference.
6. Discuss price only as research after a cost-informed range exists; no price or stock promise is authorized.
7. Close by asking what needs to be true before they would consider ordering. Do not imply a reservation exists.

## Note template

Participant code; adult consent status; interview date; recruitment source; recent buying behavior;
verbatim quote with permission; interpretation; concern; counterevidence; follow-up question; interviewer.
Keep names, contacts and audio containing identity outside Git. Use paraphrases if permission to quote is missing.

## Synthesis and decision

Group repeated needs while retaining negative cases. Separate aesthetic liking, practical suitability,
stated willingness and observed behavior. Write counts only from actual completed sessions with the denominator.
If the offer repeatedly confuses participants, revise it before production. If practical fit issues recur,
revisit the sample before more marketing. Founders set the next test based on evidence, not a made-up pass rate.
Next action: review the interview guide together and conduct a pilot before broad recruitment.
