# Manifesto alternatives

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

These short drafts are PROPOSALS for tone testing, not approved campaign copy or statements of proven quality.

## Draft A: deliberate presence

We choose what we carry into the street. A shape. A mark. A story that feels like ours.
ROGUE begins with the wish to make that choice visible. There is room for a quieter kind of defiance.

## Draft B: make the mark count

An idea becomes real through the details. We want every line to have a reason and every piece to earn its place.
We are building ROGUE one decision at a time. The story starts with what we make and what we learn.

## Draft C: room for your own expression

You bring your own meaning. We bring an invitation to create, share and take part.
ROGUE is a name for the space we hope to build together. Belonging should leave room for difference.

FACT: community and storytelling are founder aspirations. UNKNOWN: which words feel natural to intended buyers.
Test comprehension and emotional interpretation in English; ask a fluent reviewer to adapt any French or
Tunisian-Arabic version. Translation should preserve meaning, not force word-for-word slogans.
Next action: read all drafts aloud, remove phrases the founders would not actually say, and test one at a time.
