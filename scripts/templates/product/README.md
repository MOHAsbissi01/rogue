## Product album

- designs/source holds originals, including print and embroidery subfolders; create versioned derivatives.
- prototypes/v01 holds the sample record and front/back/details/fit views. Add v02, v03 and later revisions freely.
- photography has product/editorial/lifestyle plus albums for unlimited dated sessions.
- videos keeps raw, selected and approved stages separate. Raw footage stays out of Git and requires a backup.
- mockups are simulations; packaging holds working layouts; approvals records version-specific decisions.

Use `YYYY-MM-DD_PRODUCTID_view_vNN.ext` for new assets and `YYYY-MM-DD-session-name` for additional albums.
Do not rename existing originals. Record their original paths in the asset register instead.
