# Competitor research method

Owner: Both cofounders (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Purpose and boundaries

Compare 5-10 relevant alternatives using dated evidence, not reputation or invented market data.
No competitor has been researched in this setup. The eight blank matrix rows are research slots only.

PROPOSAL sample: local direct apparel/hoodie alternatives, adjacent style alternatives buyers mention,
and a few international references for creative/product comparison. Do not compare international prices
with local offers without noting currency, shipping, taxes and market context. No competitor is preselected.

## Collection protocol

1. Select each brand for a stated relevance reason. Record source URL and observation date.
2. Capture the exact garment, listed currency/price, discount status and availability at that time.
3. Record material/GSM/fit only if explicitly stated, and distinguish claims from verified performance.
4. Describe design positioning using visible product/brand language with attribution; do not copy artwork.
5. Observe engagement with platform, content sample, period and denominators where available.
   Public likes/comments are not sales, profitable demand or proof of authentic audience quality.
6. Separate observed differentiators from your interpretation. Note missing information and uncertainty.
7. Compare the same dimensions across all rows; retain links or permitted reference captures for verification.

## Founder review

Ask: which need is already well served; what tradeoff is visible; what does ROGUE's actual sample offer;
which claimed difference can be proved; what is still unknown? Avoid declaring a market gap from a small sample.
Next action: shortlist candidates from interviews, then fill evidence rows before drawing positioning conclusions.
