# Creative direction

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL: make the garment readable inside a dark, cinematic, urban and restrained visual world.
FACT: the aesthetic is gothic-inspired. UNKNOWN: approved art, locations, equipment, casting and budget.

Build each piece around one emotion, one visible product detail and one action. Use real fabric texture,
purposeful camera movement and pauses. Pair mood footage with neutral views whenever shoppers need evidence.
Avoid borrowed logos, occult/religious symbols without contextual consideration, fake crowds and fabricated workshop scenes.

Real footage is the product fidelity reference. Editor-produced sequences may rearrange approved footage while
preserving truthful claims. AI may support atmosphere or experiments, but garment shape, art, seams, color and
fit must be compared with the real prototype. Synthetic output is not manufacturing or fit evidence.
Next action: approve one concept and its exact product reference before a shoot or paid generation.
