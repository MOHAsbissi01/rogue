# Storytelling framework

Owner: Brand/Growth cofounder (assignment pending)  
Approval status: PROPOSAL - awaiting founder review  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL sequence: hook -> tension/question -> real detail -> change in understanding -> specific invitation.
For a 15-30-second piece keep one question, one story turn and one CTA. Tell the viewer why the detail matters.

| Beat | Useful question | Evidence rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | What will someone want to understand? | No false scarcity or unearned quality claim |
| Tension | What design or identity choice is being explored? | Do not invent a founder event |
| Detail | What can the viewer actually see? | Use the real garment or label a conceptual rendering |
| Turn | What does this reveal about ROGUE? | Separate fact from intention |
| Invitation | What can someone do now? | Match real availability and approved channel |

Draft copy in English, then create separately reviewed French/Tunisian-Arabic variants when needed.
Review whether the narrative survives without music and whether captions remain legible on mobile.
