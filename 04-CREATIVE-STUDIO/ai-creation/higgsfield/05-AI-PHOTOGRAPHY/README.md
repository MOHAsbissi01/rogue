# AI-assisted photography

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

Use editorial, lifestyle, backgrounds and campaign-posters for clearly labeled working categories; approved holds
only version-specific cleared derivatives or links. No image is generated or approved in this setup.

For commercial product images link the exact SKU and real front/back/details/fit references, preserving original
garment design. Proposed lower-risk route: authentic product photography on a generated background through a verified
composite. Check scale, edges, contact shadows, color and perspective. Do not invent unseen garment surfaces, embroidery,
print, fit or quality. Poster typography/offer should be assembled from approved facts in a controlled editor.

Log image attempts alongside video attempts using media_type=image and actual debit/cost references. Keep rejected
outputs for diagnosis in appropriate local storage, and retain rights/consent evidence. Next action: decide whether
AI adds useful atmosphere without misleading the customer; real product fidelity is mandatory either way.
