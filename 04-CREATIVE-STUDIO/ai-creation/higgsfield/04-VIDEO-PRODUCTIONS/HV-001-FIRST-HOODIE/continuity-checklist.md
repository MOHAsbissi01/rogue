# Continuity review - HV-001

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

- [ ] Same approved character/likeness and wardrobe reference across connected shots.
- [ ] Exact hoodie graphics, text, placement, sleeves, hood, fit and color remain faithful.
- [ ] Start/end action, screen direction, eyeline, hands, props and contact match adjacent shots.
- [ ] Light, environment, lens feel and spatial relationships support one scene/story.
- [ ] No impossible walk, frozen action, sliding feet, morphing or flicker in normal/slow playback.
- [ ] Music/ambience/voice transitions carry across cuts without accidental silence or discontinuity.

All items NOT REVIEWED. Record timecode, reference, observed problem, severity, owner, fix and retest evidence.
The editor must view a complete assembled cut; individual scene approval cannot establish sequence continuity.
