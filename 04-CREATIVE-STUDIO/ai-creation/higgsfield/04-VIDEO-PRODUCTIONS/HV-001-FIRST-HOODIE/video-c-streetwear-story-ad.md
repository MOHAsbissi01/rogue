## Synopsis, audience and emotional angle

A fictional adult character hesitates at a choice of route, then moves decisively through connected urban shots.
A tactile real hoodie detail anchors the emotional turn. The final frame resolves the action into a clear approved
offer. The story expresses self-direction without implying clothing transforms identity or guarantees belonging.

PROPOSAL audience: viewers with an existing interest in darker streetwear and a practical reason to consider a
hoodie. Refine through interviews and organic validation. Emotional angle: hesitation becoming intention. Do not
use pressure, shame or fake scarcity. Paid audience settings, eligibility and available account are UNVERIFIED.

Purpose: Paid advertising. Planned content use: Paid. Hook: One decision changes the direction of the walk.
