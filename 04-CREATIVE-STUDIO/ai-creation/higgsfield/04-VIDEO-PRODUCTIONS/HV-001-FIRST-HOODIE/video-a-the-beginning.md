## Synopsis, audience and emotional angle

A fictional adult protagonist pauses in an urban passage, notices a trace of original creative work, and chooses
to move toward a shared creative space. The hoodie carries individual expression through the movement. The ending
invites viewers to follow a brand being built, without pretending a large community already exists.

PROPOSAL audience: people drawn to darker streetwear and individual expression; test 17-28 first, 29-35 second.
The broad founder intent remains 14-35 and is not validation. Use consenting adults for this pilot. Emotional angle:
uncertainty becoming self-directed presence and an invitation to belong on one's own terms.

Purpose: Brand awareness. Planned content use: Organic. Hook: A quiet pause becomes a deliberate first step.
