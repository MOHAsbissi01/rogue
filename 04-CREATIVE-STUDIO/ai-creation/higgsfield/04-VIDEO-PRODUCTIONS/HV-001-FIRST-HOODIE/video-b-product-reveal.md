## Synopsis, audience and emotional angle

A precise visual sequence answers the viewer's practical question: what is this hoodie? It moves from a real
texture detail to front and back views, the actual decoration and a short authentic fit movement, ending with an
invitation to inspect verified product information. The turn is from atmosphere to clear physical evidence.

PROPOSAL audience: potential hoodie buyers who need to judge graphic placement, silhouette and details before
considering the offer. Age bands remain research hypotheses. Emotional angle: curiosity becoming clarity and confidence
in what is shown; do not claim confidence as a measured outcome.

Purpose: Product launch. Planned content use: Hybrid. Hook: Before the story, see the actual hoodie.
