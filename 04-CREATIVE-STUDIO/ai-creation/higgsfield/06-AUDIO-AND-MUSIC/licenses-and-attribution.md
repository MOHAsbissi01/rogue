# Audio rights register worksheet

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

For every music, effect and voice asset record: safe asset ID, title/source/creator, original URL, version,
license/consent evidence code, date verified, organic/paid permissions, platforms, territory, term/expiry,
allowed edits, attribution wording and placement, distribution restrictions and actual reviewer.

A platform library permission may be limited to that platform or use; verify rather than assume portability.
AI audio requires review of the actual provider terms and voice/likeness permissions. Do not clone a person's voice
without specific authorization and consent. Keep signed agreements and identifying files outside Git.
UNKNOWN rights block final approval; 'royalty-free' by itself is not a complete permitted-use record.
Next action: fill the cue sheet and clear each source before making a finished mixed export.
