# Proposed music and mix direction

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL mood: restrained dark pulse, tactile urban ambience and an emotional resolution. Keep the soundtrack
specific to the story rather than using generic dramatic hits on every cut. Track, artist, tempo and license are UNKNOWN.

Opening: establish a believable space and hook sound. Middle: build tension with a clear rhythmic or tonal change.
Product turn: synchronize a subtle cloth/step/contact cue to real action. Ending: resolve the score beneath legible
CTA and leave an intentional tail. For voiceover, leave space in the music and check intelligibility on a phone.

Do not assume a sound-alike, platform trend or AI-generated track grants commercial rights. Check actual output
rights and terms at use. Record source, stems, edits and mix version. Listen through full start/end and transitions;
fix abrupt cuts, clipping, unwanted artifacts and accidental silence. No loudness or platform spec is asserted here.
