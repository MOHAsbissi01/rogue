# Eight-stage production workflow

Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed

## Gates and handoffs

| Stage | Concrete work | Exit evidence | Responsible role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Creative strategy | Choose awareness, launch, emotional story, organic engagement, paid advertising or conversion | Approved objective, audience hypothesis, metric and CTA feasibility | Creative lead + marketing owner |
| 2 Story development | Define protagonist, setting, tension, action, turn, product role and final message | Treatment that can be understood without invented brand history | Director / writer |
| 3 Visual preproduction | Moodboard, canonical references, timed storyboard, shots, light, camera, wardrobe and audio | Reference lock, continuity plan, rights and finishing owner | Director + product reviewer + editor |
| 4 Generation | Verify workflow, settings, reference support, duration/aspect and authorization; submit only when authorized | Every attempt/job/settings/outcome and debit/refund recorded | AI operator |
| 5 Assembly and sound | Select, connect and trim scenes; mix music/SFX/voice; titles, logo, end card and CTA | Complete versioned master and editable timeline/manifest | Assigned editor/finisher |
| 6 QA | Story, movement, fidelity, continuity, sound, rights, crop, readability, artifacts and export playback | Timecoded review with defects and retest evidence | Product + creative + rights reviewers |
| 7 Cofounder approval | Review exact master and derivatives; sign scope, version, channels and conditions | Actual approvals with ISO dates; no automatic acceptance | Both cofounders |
| 8 Measurement | After separately authorized publication, link posts/ads and collect actual results | Defined windows/sources, denominator, learning and decision | Marketing + analytics owner |

## State transitions

IDEA is a proposal. BRIEFED means objective/story/reference requirements and owners are agreed; it does not
authorize uploads or paid calls. GENERATING requires explicit authorization and a passed reference/rights gate.
EDITING requires usable source scenes or authentic substitutes and an accepted finishing owner. REVIEW means a
complete playable cut exists. APPROVED requires cofounder evidence for exact versions. PUBLISHED requires an actual
authorized release and post/ad record. A status cannot substitute for missing evidence. Use blocker and approval
columns alongside state. Keep all approval history when revisions return work to EDITING or REVIEW.

Provider-native scene assembly, audio, captions, transitions and export are UNVERIFIED in this setup. Route each
task through the capability matrix at execution. If unsupported, assign a supplementary editor with a reviewed
handoff; manual tasks are review, factual confirmation, rights clearance and final signoff. No founders-only
editing burden should be concealed behind 'generation complete'.
