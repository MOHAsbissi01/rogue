# Production cost and resource calculation framework

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

**Total Video Production Cost = Higgsfield Usage + External Tools + Editing + Audio + Other Direct Costs.**
All amounts remain blank until verified inputs are provided. This is a written calculation framework with editable
CSV records, not an automatically recalculating workbook. Currency for local reporting is TND.

## Build a scoped estimate

Define production/deliverables, planned shot coverage, image/video/upscale/audio operations, revisions, finishing
tasks and permitted attempts. Planning counts are proposals, not observed generation limits. Obtain current
workflow/plan evidence and real editor/audio/tool quotes. Record estimates and actuals distinctly in basis/status.
Do not estimate TND from credits with an invented exchange rate. Monetary conversion from another currency needs
source amount, actual currency, dated FX basis and fx_rate_to_tnd; amount_tnd = source_amount x verified rate.
An actual TND source amount can be recorded directly. Taxes/payment/FX fees require applicable verified treatment.

## Credits and subscription allocation

Log each actual attempt, including unsuccessful image/video/upscale/audio jobs and revisions. Join provider debit
and refund events by attempt/job ID; net credits = verified debits - verified refunds for the same scope. Count
failed/rejected outcomes from real attempt rows only, not TEMPLATE. Unknown charges/refunds remain unresolved.

Subscription usage cost needs a documented allocation policy and actual subscription amount/period; credits may
be a chosen allocation driver only with a justified complete denominator and eligible pool. This is allocation,
not a universal credit-to-money exchange. Avoid counting both subscription allocation and the same included
credits as a second charge. Include purchased top-ups/direct charges only once on their real basis. Track cost of
failed attempts and revisions; they are not free because they were rejected. Reconcile total allocated costs to
the verified period expense and identify any unallocated share.

## Cost per finished video

Sum in-scope actual cost lines across the five categories once. Cost per approved deliverable = total allocated
production cost / verified approved-deliverable count when that count is positive and all required costs are known.
Count distinct approved videos under the agreed scope; do not count each platform encode as a new video unless the
report explicitly defines it that way. Shared A/B/C costs need a documented allocation, not three duplicate charges.
No approved deliverables exist in this record; do not calculate a zero or misleading average. Cash paid and cost
incurred are separate dates. Final approval and publication are separate from production spending authorization.

Before commitment: both founders review scope, quotes, allocation, credit/monetary ceiling, cash impact and expiry.
After delivery: reconcile ledger events, attempt failures/revisions, actual editor/audio costs and approved output
count. Report missing evidence and planned-vs-actual differences without inventing a profitability conclusion.
