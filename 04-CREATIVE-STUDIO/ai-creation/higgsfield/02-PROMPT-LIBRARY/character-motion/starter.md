## Prompt draft

Start with feet planted. The subject shifts weight naturally, takes two unhurried steps, stops and turns their head toward the off-screen cue. Keep hand contact and cloth movement plausible. Avoid changing the hoodie silhouette or graphics.

## Use and review

Inspect feet, knees, hands and cloth at normal and slow playback; camera motion alone is insufficient.

Before use fill shot ID, canonical reference, verified workflow/settings, rights and authorization. Record exact prompt/version and each attempt; no durations, costs or results are assumed.
