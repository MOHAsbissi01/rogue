# Tracking that explains action

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

**PROPOSAL - UNTESTED.**

## Prompt draft

Camera tracks parallel to the subject at a steady walking pace, maintaining a medium view and stable height. No orbit, zoom or abrupt perspective jump. End in a stationary frame with room for the next cut.

## Use and review

Specify subject action separately; test camera and subject changes independently.

Before use fill shot ID, canonical reference, verified workflow/settings, rights and authorization. Record exact prompt/version and each attempt; no durations, costs or results are assumed.
