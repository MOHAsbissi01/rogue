An authorized account owner should record a safe plan label, verification date, official source/secure evidence code,
billing period and allowed workflows. Redact personal information. Log each provider debit, refund, grant, expiry or
adjustment as its own event with attempt/job reference and actual evidence. Failed outputs may still consume credits;
a refund remains unknown until verified. Credit use and money are different measurements.

Before each authorized batch: reconcile verified balance, proposed scope and credit/spend ceilings; verify reference
rights and record authorization. Afterward: record actual events and reconcile closing = opening + grants + refunds
- debits - expiries + signed adjustments on a consistent period basis. Unknown events block reconciliation.

Do not assume credits convert to TND at a fixed rate. Subscription allocation, paid top-ups and direct charges need
separate documented monetary bases, avoiding duplicate allocation. Next action: the founder/account owner provides
safe verified inputs when ready; this documentation makes no subscription or spending commitment.
