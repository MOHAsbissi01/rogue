# Production quality-control checklist

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

All checks start NOT REVIEWED. Copy a timecoded review record into the production and link the exact master/version.

- [ ] Hook and story are intelligible; the protagonist's action causes a meaningful turn and resolves into the CTA.
- [ ] Product matches all canonical front/back/detail/fit references throughout, including graphic text and placement.
- [ ] Character, garment, prop, light, geography and screen direction stay coherent across connected shots.
- [ ] Subject movement is visible where planned; anatomy, contact, walking and cloth motion are believable.
- [ ] No unwanted text, logos, extra limbs, melted details, flicker or interpolation artifacts remain.
- [ ] A complete assembled edit exists; the founder is not handed an unordered folder of clips as final delivery.
- [ ] Music, ambience, effects and any voice are present as planned; no accidental silence, clipping or abrupt tail.
- [ ] Music/voice/likeness/art/font/location rights cover exact organic/paid use, territory, duration and adaptations.
- [ ] Any needed branded-content/synthetic-media disclosures are reviewed for the intended use.
- [ ] Titles, subtitles, logo and CTA are readable on an actual mobile preview and do not obscure crucial product details.
- [ ] Exact duration, aspect, dimensions, fps, codec and audio stream are checked in the exported file; playback completes.
- [ ] Offer, price, availability, claims and destination match approved facts; no false urgency or unsupported quality claim.
- [ ] Cofounder approvals name version/hash, channels, date and conditions; revisions were retested.

Defect record: issue ID; master path/version; timecode; expected vs observed; severity; owner; fix; evidence; actual
reviewer/date. Product inaccuracy, missing required sound, unclear story, absent rights or broken export block delivery.
No automatic checklist completion, quality score or pass is assigned by folder creation.
