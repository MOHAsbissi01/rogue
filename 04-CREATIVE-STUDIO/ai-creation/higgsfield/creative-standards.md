# Creative standards

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

Each production needs one objective, a viewer question, a connected story and a product role. Atmosphere supports
meaning; it cannot replace garment evidence or a clear final action. PROPOSAL mood: gothic, cinematic, rebellious,
urban, atmospheric and premium-looking. Premium-looking describes art direction, not proven material quality or price.

## Acceptance standard

| Dimension | Required evidence | Reject or revise when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story | Hook, setup, action, turn, resolution and CTA visible in the assembled cut | Shots could be shuffled with no effect on meaning |
| Garment | Shot-to-reference comparison for all visible details | Logo, placement, color, sleeves, hood, fit or graphic changes |
| Motion | Intentional body action plus planned camera behavior | Only a camera push on a frozen person or implausible limb/cloth motion |
| Continuity | Stable person, garment, light and screen direction across connected shots | A cut silently changes identity, art, scene geography or action |
| Sound | Reviewed music/effects mix; speech if relevant; rights evidence | Silent accident, clipping, abrupt tail or unlicensed track |
| Delivery | Full reviewed master with titles/end card/CTA and destination variants | Unassembled clips or unfinished audio are called final |

Assign creative lead, product reviewer, editor/finisher, rights reviewer and cofounder approvers by role before
production. One person may hold several roles, but review responsibilities must be explicit. Next action: choose
the brief and finishing owner, then review the storyboard and sound plan together.
