# Cinematic style guide

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

PROPOSAL: use dark surroundings, shaped light, urban texture and controlled camera movement while keeping the
actual garment readable. Contrast supports attention; underexposure must not hide product detail.

Plan establishing geography, medium action, a detail that matters, and a resolved final frame. Specify motivated
movement: a walk, pause, turn or reaching action belongs to the story. A camera orbit alone does not solve static
character behavior. Match direction, lens feel, light and action across cuts. Keep clothing color consistent within
the actual light rather than grading it into a fictional colorway.

Choose one dominant movement per shot, leave edit handles where possible, and align the final pose with the next
shot's opening. Textural ambience and a restrained licensed score should support tension and resolution. Next action:
build a timed storyboard and sound cue sheet before requesting any generation or production expense.
