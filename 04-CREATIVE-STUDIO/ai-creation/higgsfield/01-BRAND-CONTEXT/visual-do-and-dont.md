# Visual do and do not

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Preserve exact real graphic placement, lettering and proportions | Let a generated logo or sleeve art drift because the image looks good |
| Show believable action with cause and response | Count moving smoke or a zoom on a frozen person as character action |
| Link shots through matching action, direction and garment | Assemble unrelated attractive clips into an incoherent story |
| Make texture and silhouette visible | Hide incorrect details in darkness, blur, rapid cuts or compression |
| Use licensed music, deliberate sound and a resolved ending | Deliver accidental silence or assume a prompt produced audio |
| Use original references with documented permissions | Borrow another brand's artwork or unlicensed likeness |
| Separate fictional atmosphere from verified product evidence | Present imaginary workshops, customers or sample tests as fact |
| Review a complete mobile-ready master | Hand founders disconnected scenes and call production finished |

Any actual exception requires a reason and reviewer; product fabrication is not an acceptable exception.
