# Recurring storytelling themes

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

These are PROPOSALS, not approved campaigns or founder biography.

| Theme | Dramatic action | Product role | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finding a personal direction | A character pauses, chooses a path and moves with intent | Actual hoodie worn in an everyday urban situation | Fictional character story must not become a false founder event |
| An idea becomes tangible | Original design detail is compared with the real sample | Garment makes a design decision visible | Use existing genuine sketches and real process records |
| Belonging by choice | Individuals approach a shared creative activity | Common piece with distinct personal expression | Do not portray invented customers as an established community |
| Details earn attention | A viewer's question is answered by a closer real view | Product evidence carries the narrative turn | No durability/material claim from visual appearance alone |

Connect one theme to an audience question and measurable outcome. Avoid visual sequences with no change in
understanding or emotion. Next action: choose which theme the first-hoodie brief should test.
