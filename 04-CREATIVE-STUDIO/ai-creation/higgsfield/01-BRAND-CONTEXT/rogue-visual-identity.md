# ROGUE visual context

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

FACT: ROGUE is Tunisian gothic-inspired streetwear beginning with hoodies. Two cofounders aspire to community,
story, recognizable mindset and good quality at a fair price. Quality and fair price remain goals.
The continuation brief adds a premium-looking, rebellious, urban and atmospheric creative direction.

Use the existing identity guidelines as the source of truth for logos, palette, type and voice. No new logo,
font, colorway or slogan is approved by this folder. The proposed dark palette is an identity exploration,
not a statement of garment color. Avoid generic gothic symbols that lack an intentional brand meaning.

Direction to test: restrained darkness with readable cloth, deliberate motion and emotionally grounded action.
Use authentic Tunisian context when actually captured and permission-cleared; do not invent local location facts
or imply generated scenery documents a real place. Next action: choose a story direction and approved visual references.
