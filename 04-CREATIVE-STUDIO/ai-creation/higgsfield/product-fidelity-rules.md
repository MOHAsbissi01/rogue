# Mandatory product fidelity standard

Owner: Creative lead (cofounder assignment pending)  
Status: PROPOSAL - not approved; no production executed  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

ROGUE sells physical clothes. Never intentionally change, hallucinate or redesign the hoodie in commercial imagery.
A visually convincing wrong garment is a rejected output. Real product originals must never be overwritten.

## Reference gate before commercial generation

Require the exact approved commercial SKU/variant and source product record; original neutral front/back photographs;
close-ups of every actual graphic, print and embroidery area; approved accurate color reference; visible fabric texture;
verified regular/oversized/other fit specification; measurements and approved variations; logo placement, sleeves, hood,
trims, seams and decoration scale. If a feature does not exist, mark NOT APPLICABLE only after actual review.
No product color, fit, GSM or decoration method is assumed for RG-H001. Its organizational ID alone is insufficient.

Use relative links to canonical files in 03-PRODUCTS/collections. Capture path/version, optional SHA-256, view, lighting,
rights, source date and reviewer. The photo's appearance is not proof of composition or quality. Create clearly named
reference derivatives if needed; retain originals and log transformations. Missing critical views block commercial shots.

## Timecoded review

Compare full front/back views and every interval where the garment is visible, including transitions and interpolated
frames. Check graphic wording/orientation/scale, placement relative to seams, sleeve sides, logo, hood geometry, trims,
color under lighting, silhouette, proportion and plausible fabric response. Inspect start/end frames and movement at
normal and slow playback. Texturing cannot conceal design drift. Record timecode, reference, discrepancy, severity,
required change, reviewer and retest result. Material discrepancies block APPROVED.

If fidelity fails, pause and diagnose reference quality or workflow limits. Use authentic product footage or a verified
composite preserving original garment pixels, with occlusion, light and motion reviewed. Any composite still requires
frame-level QA. AI background-only work is often a proposed lower-risk route, not a guaranteed capability. Never publish
a fabricated detail as real or describe a planned product variant as available. Log every rejected attempt and its cost.
